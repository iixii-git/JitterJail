Usage, Guide & Tips

Using JitterJail Every Day

From initial Bluetooth pairing to fine-tuning filter type and damping strength: here you'll find the demo video, LED status overview, and practical tips for daily use.

JitterJail in Action (Before / After)

Without JitterJail (Off) Tremor-induced zigzag movements and accidental right-clicks make precise selection in Excel difficult.
With JitterJail (On) A single short button press is all it takes: the cursor glides smoothly across cells and selects accurately without misclicks.

Quick Guide: Function Settings & LED Status

Quick Guide (PDF)
1 Power On & Connect Mouse
  • Power On (4 sec) – LED blinks White:
    Right after plugging in, JitterJail waits 4 seconds for your choice.
  • Press button → LED Purple (Pairing Mode):
    For a new Bluetooth mouse. Start pairing on the mouse.
  • Wait 4 sec → LED Cyan (Reconnect):
    Automatically reconnects to your already paired mouse.
2 Normal Operation (Mouse Connected)
  • LED Red – Spring Filter Active:
    Gentle, spring-like damping of tremor movements.
  • LED Blue – 1-Euro Filter Active:
    Dynamic smoothing for both slow and fast movements.
  • LED Green – Off (Bypass):
    Filter paused; mouse and right click work completely normally.
Short press (< 2 sec) toggles directly between ON (Red/Blue) and OFF / Bypass (Green).
3 Adjust Filter & Damping: Hold button for 2 sec

During normal operation, hold the button for 2 seconds to set the filter type and damping strength one after another:

Menu 1: Choose Filter Type

Color changes every 3 sec
Red = Spring Filter Blue = 1-Euro Filter

→ Confirm with a short press on desired color and continue to Menu 2.

Menu 2: Choose Damping Strength

Color changes every 2 sec
Green = Light Blue = Medium Red = Strong

→ Save & activate with a short press on desired color.

Important Note: How the Right Mouse Button Works
  • With damping active (LED Red & Blue): Hold the right mouse button for 0.5 to 1.5 seconds (after 0.5 sec, the cursor briefly twitches as confirmation) and then release – the right-click triggers on release. Shorter accidental clicks are filtered out.
  • In Bypass mode (LED Green): The right mouse button responds immediately as usual with no hold time required.

Tips for the Best Setup

Spring or 1-Euro Filter?

The 1-Euro Filter (Blue) adapts dynamically: it keeps the cursor steady while aiming, yet stays responsive across wide screen moves. The Spring Filter (Red) smooths evenly like a subtle counterweight.

Use the Right-Click Twitch

No need to count seconds: press and hold the right mouse button and simply wait for the brief visual cursor twitch after half a second – then release right away.

Quick Bypass for Other Users

If someone else briefly uses your computer or you need right-button drag-and-drop, a short press (< 2 sec) switches the filter to Green (Bypass) – and just as quickly back.
