About the Creator

Who Is Behind JitterJail?

Hi! I'm Stephan, 53 years old, an IT consultant and passionate tinkerer.

Six years ago, I was diagnosed with Parkinson's disease. Since I spend all day at a computer in my job, I know firsthand how exhausting it is to repeatedly aim at small buttons with a tremor until the click finally lands.

To ease the effects of tremor, I tried plenty of software tools. While they generally work, they all have a catch: they cost money – sometimes quite a lot. On top of that, restricted corporate networks often don't allow installing software at all, or the program isn't available for the operating system in use.

That is exactly why I made it my mission to make a helpful tool like this freely available to everyone without barriers.

That was the birth of JitterJail!

OS Independent

Works regardless of operating system – even in locked-down networks without install permissions.

Hardware-Based

Ready to use as a standalone hardware solution, with no extra drivers or background apps needed.

Free

Built from personal experience and made accessible to everyone – with no license fees or subscriptions.
